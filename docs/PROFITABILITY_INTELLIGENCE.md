# Profitability Intelligence V1
Owner-only report, migration 039. Current latest ledger revision approved+paid (pending and reversed excluded), actual receipt amount or latest immutable replacement, no double counting. UTC transaction date and expense date, not matching cohorts or cash settlement. Negative recorded contribution supported. Expenses optionally allocated wholly to one documented channel and reviewed campaign; no splits, arbitrary matching, bank confirmation or complete cost claim. Expense correction uses optimistic revision expectation and locks receipt; zero voids, no deletion. Product/campaign/source/retailer labels based on explicit latest attribution and cost allocation, unknown included, up to 51 groups per view. Different views partition same totals; no cross-view addition or verified product net profit. Retailer group is historical campaign label, not stable ID. Scenario calculator uses integer cents and basis points, floors expected realized commission and maximum acquisition cost per outbound click, no forecasts or actual EPC. No spending or automatic scale decisions. Requires authentic affiliate statements, cost and attribution evidence, complete costs, bank reconciliation, matching click cohort and approved budget for real profitability decisions.
