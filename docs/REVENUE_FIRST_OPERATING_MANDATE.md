# Dealwovo — Revenue-first operating mandate

## Purpose
Dealwovo is a merchandising and customer acquisition business. Its objective is sustainable net contribution from genuine qualifying affiliate sales, followed by selective dropshipping only after validated demand and economics. Software, agents, dashboards and integrations are enabling tools, not business outcomes.

## Binding priority order
1. Obtain real affiliate partner and advertiser approvals, permitted sourcing and promotional-channel terms, tracking identifiers and payout arrangements. Use the existing import and connection workflows; do not build another activation dashboard unless a demonstrated blocker requires it.
2. Research product demand, customer purchase intent, gifting and seasonal relevance, retailer reliability, competition, price and actual partner commission. Record source, observation date, geography and uncertainties. No inferred popularity or estimated commission presented as verified.
3. Prepare the first 100 distinct commercially eligible products as a target, not a reason to delay a smaller viable launch. Start with a pilot collection of 10–20 high-intent products from authorized sources, review actual rights, variants, stock, prices, destinations and attribution terms. No fake listings, unauthorized scraping, self-purchases or invented sales.
4. Merchandise the assortment around customer intent: current holiday deals and gift discovery in balance, useful trending finds and evergreen essentials. Department taxonomy stays distinct from overlapping editorial collections. Initial allocation hypothesis: 25 seasonal, 25 gift discovery, 20 gadgets, 15 home/kitchen, 10 essentials, 5 beauty; count unique eligible products separately, allow overlap and revise based on evidence. These are targets, not demand forecasts.
5. Validate a mobile-friendly purchase journey, truthful claims, disclosures, actual network attribution and commercial release gates. Obtain independent owner approval before production promotion. Do not expose draft products or held affiliate links.
6. Create product-specific organic social and search content, publish only after explicit channel/rights approval, measure qualified traffic and outbound clicks. Avoid paid acquisition until approved spend limits and observed contribution economics support it.
7. Reconcile actual network transactions, approved commission, reversals and paid statements. Paid network status is not bank settlement. Track attributable expenses and contribution; improve weak collections, copy, placements and acquisition channels based on observed data.

## Commercial scorecard
Leading indicators: distinct eligible products, source coverage, current verified offers, qualified sessions, outbound click-through rate, and partner-reported attributed orders. Lagging indicators: qualifying conversion rate, approved commissions net of reversals, network-paid commissions, verified cash received, costs and net contribution. Record denominator, measurement window, coverage and unknowns. Do not label clicks as sales, projections as results or internal review as external approval.

## Change rule
Prioritize existing tools and real commercial execution. Defer feature expansion, additional agents and administrative interfaces unless they directly remove a revenue, trust, compliance or operational blocker. Keep paid AI, email, advertising and production release on their existing authorization holds. Preserve the current shopping experience and year-round balanced holiday strategy. Revenue goals never override partner terms, customer trust or security.
