# US holiday merchandising research — 2026-11-24
NRF/Prosper October holiday survey: 56% of holiday shoppers report economic conditions affect spending plans; clothing (44%), books/media (25%) and beauty/personal care (24%) appear on wish lists. The NRF November 19 Thanksgiving weekend survey estimates roughly 183 million intended shoppers. These are survey-level intent signals, not observed SKU sales or actual commission. Source: https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/affordability-shapes-winter-holiday-shopping-according-to-nrf-holiday-survey and https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/183-million-consumers-expected-to-shop-during-thanksgiving-weekend .
Priority hypotheses: affordable useful gifts, personal care, practical accessories, seasonal gift discovery. Electronics, home, kitchen, pet and travel candidates are exploratory diversification, not proven trending. Fifteen candidate classes in migration 035; no invented product URLs, actual price, commission or availability. Verify model/variant, partner approval, real rate, search evidence, licensed imagery and current price before selection. Avoid gift cards until partner terms and commission are verified. Start with 10–20 authorized offers. NRF research is context, not a product-level scored observation.
