# Shopper product comparison V1
Compare 2–4 eligible retailer offers from Shop, homepage catalog or assistant. Product detail can open up to four offers of that catalog product. URL contains only offer UUIDs, no shopper identity; selections on each source page are temporary, not cross-page persisted. Compare server re-reads requested IDs using anonymous RLS, validates UUIDs and cap, projects only public fields, and omits expired or inaccessible records. Client refreshes expiry at focus and every 30 seconds. Existing outbound endpoint rechecks current eligibility at click. Recorded price range/difference only for same catalog product with positive USD prices and at least two distinct retailer names; not guaranteed savings or independently matched variants. No stock, shipping, product specification or popularity claims. Unknown prices shown explicitly. Responsive horizontally scrollable table. Separate Amazon editorial links excluded. No new migration, paid service, or account tracking. Synthetic regression suite in build; actual product inventory and browser acceptance pending.
