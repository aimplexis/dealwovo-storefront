# Merchandising V1
Automatic, evidence-limited collections use latest 100 eligible general catalog offers (anonymous RLS), recheck review/expiry in client, deduplicate products, deterministic recency then offer ID. Home recent finds, tech/essentials/holiday pages, four buying guides and related product detail rails. Gift/seasonal/guide matches require explicit catalog text and/or department; under-$25 requires positive recorded USD below 2500 cents; related offers are same category and ranked by shared title tokens, not proven equivalent. Empty/unavailable states avoid fabricated listings. Existing daily promotion remains independent. No demand ranking, commission-based steering, tracking, paid placements, user profiling or external fetch; actual source authorization still required. Snapshot limited to 100 offers; no guarantee of full catalog coverage. Current product status changes are rechecked at outbound route; client refresh on focus and expiry timer. Amazon editorial not part of price-bearing rails. Regression tests synthetic. Next: product-specific campaign preparation and attribution.
