## Candidate policy
Consider non-electrical desk and drawer organizers, packing organizers, decorative accessories and simple travel accessories first. Review material safety, dimensions, intellectual property and shipment damage. Food-contact products require evidence; exclude children’s goods, batteries, electrical devices, ingestibles, regulated medical goods and high-risk cosmetics initially. No supplier or item is approved here.

## Unit economics
Per-order contribution = net item revenue excluding collected tax minus supplier item cost, shipping, payment fees, packaging/handling, expected returns/refunds net of recoveries, chargebacks, support and acquisition costs. Avoid double counting returned revenue. Track fixed overhead separately. Compare with affiliate expected commission using same traffic cohort and risk-adjusted assumptions. Stress test higher shipping, lower conversion, supplier price increases and return losses. Unknown costs mean HOLD. Never use generic margin assumptions as quotes.
Proposed gates: positive contribution under base and downside assumptions, documented working-capital reserve covering refunds/chargebacks and fulfillment before payout, approved order cap and loss limit. Exact numeric thresholds require owner approval.
