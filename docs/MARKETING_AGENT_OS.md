# Dealwovo Marketing Agent OS: v1

Five specialist roles: Campaign Strategist, Content Studio, SEO Guardian, Trust/Claims Guardian, and Performance Analyst, coordinated through private automated weekly drafts.

The Supabase planner `dealwovo_private.plan_marketing_week()` generates four private channel drafts on the configured weekly pg_cron schedule (Monday 14:00 UTC). The owner can additionally generate a manual batch (limited to once every 30 minutes) and mark individual drafts internally reviewed or archived. Owner review NEVER publishes a post. This requires a separately authorized social or CMS connector and explicit release policy.

All draft sources are restricted to observed Dealwovo editorial pages and database counts. No external trends, retailer approval, sales attribution, follower numbers, product discounts, stock, or pricing claims are invented. Customer information is never used. Post copy is deterministic rules-based, not yet generative AI; no paid model call or service has been authorized. Future generative creativity can use an approved AI Gateway budget, grounded data retrieval, opt-in and human publication checkpoints.

No emails, SMS, DMs, social posts, CMS edits, SEO-indexing enablement or ad spending are automated. This previews the marketing orchestration foundation, not a launch-ready growth engine. Supabase leaked-password protection, actual affiliate partnerships, customer consent, social integrations, image licensing and analytics remain separate gates. Production domain and `main` remain unchanged.
