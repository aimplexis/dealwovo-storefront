# Smart Shopping Assistant V1
Public `/assistant` route; guided catalog matching, no LLM, network search, paid service, user profiles or persistent shopping conversation. Anonymous Supabase RLS projection of latest 100 eligible offers, strips raw affiliate destinations. Browser excludes expired/future review timestamps, refreshes time every 30 seconds and at focus/submission. Existing product and outbound endpoints recheck eligibility. Exact normalized keyword matching across title/category/description, title matches ranked above category and description; tie by review recency. Strict explicit under vs inclusive maximum, stricter of typed/selected budget, no unknown-price/non-USD offer with budget, no unsupported negation/ranges, no silent widening. One match per product; first 8 of exact count. Gift purpose changes editorial guidance, not inferred recipient suitability. Gift Finder hands off purpose and optional upper budget. No Amazon editorial links (separate publication hold, no known price). Empty/unavailable distinction; no fake listings or invented discounts. Tests synthetic only; real catalog, browser acceptance and launch authorization still required. No database migration. Next: shopper acceptance, real product population, stronger catalog-wide search and optional consent-aware wishlist. No paid AI until approved.
