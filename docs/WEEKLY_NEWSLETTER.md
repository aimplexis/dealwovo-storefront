# Weekly Deals Newsletter V1
Public `/newsletter` is a preference-preview only; no email input or real subscription collection. Homepage/footer link to it. Private subscriber/consent tables and trusted-service-only request/confirm/unsubscribe functions, with collection disabled at database level, single-use confirmation hash and 48h expiry, separate unsubscribe hash, idempotent suppression. No reactivation or address enumeration through public routes. No public subscription/confirmation routes, provider adapter, email sending or customer contact import. Future backend must generate independent high-entropy tokens, hash before DB call, avoid logging emails/tokens, rate-limit, add confirmation and unsubscribe HTTP flows, handle pending expiration, resubscription, deletion/retention, sender identity, postal address, final privacy and partner channel policies. Owner-only internal Studio generates five interest drafts per UTC week (Monday 14:20 UTC scheduler or manual), exact eligible catalog records capped at five, no prices or invented discounts, links to own product/guide pages, immutable content and reviewed/archive states, no subscriber access or email send. Scheduled drafting is internal only; fresh link/eligibility review and delivery approval required. Tests use synthetic fixtures only; no external requests or paid services.
