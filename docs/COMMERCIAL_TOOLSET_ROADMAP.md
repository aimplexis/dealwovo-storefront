## Tool selection and costs
Start with existing GitHub/Vercel/Supabase stack; manual exports and first-party reports before paid APIs. Google Trends for relative search interest (not sales volume); Search Console for own-site search queries, impressions and clicks after verification; Amazon SiteStripe for permitted links and Creators API only with actual eligibility; Awin authorized feed and transaction reports, clickref only as allowed by advertiser and privacy policy. Evaluate paid keyword/competitive research, email delivery and behavioral analytics only after a named blocker and cost approval. No additional subscriptions, account connections, live requests or secrets created by this roadmap.

## Scoring and economics
Retain proposed demand 25, commission 20, price competitiveness 15, seasonality 15, merchant reliability 10, conversion evidence 10, availability 5. Missing inputs remain unknown, not zero or invented; show coverage, source date and limitations; do not rank unapproved products as eligible. Estimate earnings per outbound click = assumed conversion probability × average qualifying order value × applicable commission rate × (1 - expected reversal fraction); explicitly distinguish observed versus hypothetical inputs. Actual net contribution = approved earned commissions net of reversals minus attributable costs, separate cash from accrual and avoid double-counting revisions. Never guarantee revenue or profitability.
