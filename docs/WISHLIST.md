# Wishlist V1
Guest wishlist across homepage catalog, Shop, Shopping Assistant, Gift Finder, product details and Compare. Explicit Save for later only; at most 40 product UUIDs and bounded product title snapshots stored in browser localStorage, no prices/retailer links/account identity stored. Clear/remove controls, same-origin tab synchronization, temporary state fallback if storage fails. The wishlist page POSTs only saved product UUIDs to /api/wishlist (bounded 4096-byte body, strict UUID validation, no-store, no raw destination). Anonymous RLS read independently of latest-100 catalog, current eligible offers only, at most three per product. Expired offers disappear on clock check; focus and explicit refresh refetch. Missing products retained locally without stale price or checkout links. No paid AI, server-side shopper profiles, account sync, emails, background price alerts, cookies or database migration. Browser local storage is origin-specific; preview lists do not automatically transfer to production. Real catalog/browser acceptance pending; privacy notice remains prelaunch and requires final review.
