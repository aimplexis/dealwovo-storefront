# Dealwovo repository instructions

## Mandatory language policy
English (US) is the only supported language for the initial release. Write all user-facing interface copy, error messages, editorial content, generated marketing drafts, administration screens, documentation, code comments, pull request descriptions and commit messages in English. Do not introduce automatic translation, language switching or locale detection without explicit founder approval. Preserve third-party names and legitimate technical identifiers as supplied.

The root document declares en-US and requests no automatic translation. These declarations are advisory to browsers; they do not change GitHub's own interface language or override a visitor's explicit translation settings.

Before committing, review changed human-readable text for English consistency. Run npm test and npm run build; record actual results. Never claim that an automated language check proves every sentence is English.

## Year-round brand direction
Dealwovo is a welcoming shopping discovery destination for everyday life and every occasion. Prioritize useful everyday products, lifestyle inspiration, practical solutions, personal interests and meaningful life events. Holiday promotions are a seasonal collection, never the entire platform identity. Maintain a balanced year-round homepage, navigation, editorial calendar and product sourcing strategy. Do not confuse inspiration with verified inventory or claim unverified deals.

## Founder-confirmed discovery experience
The desired customer behavior is: "I don't know exactly what I'm looking for, but let me see what's new on Dealwovo today." Build a welcoming, curiosity-led, year-round destination that is useful even without a specific purchase intent. Prioritize fresh editorial discovery, genuinely useful everyday finds, meaningful occasions, shopper-controlled interests and a clear path to verified offers. Never imply daily updates or new inventory unless supported by actual content and review timestamps. Measure repeat visits and meaningful discovery only after approved measurement is available; do not use manipulative urgency, addictive engagement patterns or fabricated social proof. Favor sustainable editorial operations over speculative features.

## Founder directive — sales and customer acquisition first (2026-11-26)
Stop nonessential feature expansion, new internal dashboards, advisory worksheets, duplicate agent layers and speculative automation. Prioritize customer acquisition, affiliate click-through, genuine qualifying commissions and repeat visits. Reuse existing storefront, content, recommendation, comparison, sharing, newsletter and marketing tools before building replacements. Every proposed development task must identify its customer-facing outcome, measurable commercial objective, existing capability reused, and actual dependency. The first commercial bottleneck is authorized affiliate inventory: the latest inspected database had zero authorized sources, eligible candidates, products and offers. Prioritize a permitted 10–20 product collection and conversion-ready product pages, original search-focused buying guides, mobile shopping paths and organic distribution assets. Do not claim that planned or drafted marketing has generated traffic or sales. Essential security and release acceptance remain required, but do not turn them into unrelated feature expansion. Existing holds remain binding: no production promotion, indexing, external posting, new spending, Amazon activation or shareable preview access without separate authorization. English US only.

## Release safeguards
Read PROJECT_STATE.md before starting. Keep the preview private: do not create shareable access links or change deployment protection. ## Founder directive — outcome-driven hybrid commerce (November 26, 2026)
Follow docs/commerce/HYBRID_COMMERCE_TECHNOLOGY.md as the proposed architecture. Build a commercially operational affiliate and selective dropshipping storefront, not another collection of internal tools. Preserve the existing year-round discovery experience. For every task: identify a real commercial bottleneck, inspect existing implementation and provider prerequisites, verify current official documentation when adopting technology, implement the smallest useful integration, add regression coverage, inspect exact build results, and report observed outcomes separately from intentions. Never fabricate product data, provider access, demand, orders, revenue, test results, or deployment status. Do not add duplicate dashboards, simulated agents, speculative infrastructure, or content solely to increase feature count. Prioritize authorized affiliate inventory and first qualifying sale, then Shopify hosted checkout and approved supplier fulfillment, then measured search and distribution improvements. Stop expanding features when a provider authorization or business decision blocks the commercial critical path; state the exact owner action. Use existing tools and data first. All approval descriptions and authored text must be English US. No spending, public release, indexing, external posting, Amazon reactivation or sharing private preview access without separate authorization. Success metrics are eligible products, qualified traffic, retailer clicks, confirmed commission, paid retail orders, fulfillment quality, contribution profit and repeat visits. A commit is not a verified build or commercial result.

## Founder sourcing directive — exceptional everyday value
Dealwovo is not a cheap-product store. Source genuinely useful, reliable products at competitive affordable total customer prices. Prioritize everyday needs and substantiated American manufacturing for dropshipping; keep broader reputable affiliate sourcing. Distinguish US manufacture from US brand, assembly and shipping; require SKU-level substantiation and applicable FTC origin standards. Reuse the existing discovery evidence ledger, authorized feed intake, offer comparison and merchandising rather than creating duplicate agents or dashboards. Evaluate exact variant, pack size, condition, materials, credible quality evidence, delivered cost (including known mandatory shipping and fees), warranty, returns, supplier reliability and contribution margin. Unknown costs or quality are unknown, not zero or assumed excellent. Never assert a discount, lowest price, price advantage or origin without current comparable evidence. Commission must not override customer value or safety. Rank affordable, quality-qualified products ahead of low-quality cheap products; use actual authorized data and reject unsupported claims. No new subscriptions or live provider activation without separate approval.

## Founder clarification — broad everyday essentials
Dealwovo must discover quality, affordable essentials across all legitimate everyday shopping departments, not a narrow niche or holiday assortment. Research home, kitchen, cleaning, personal care, beauty, wellness, electronics, apparel, automotive, family, pets, office, school, tools, garden, pantry, travel, recreation, household safety, hobbies and occasions. Reuse the existing taxonomy and discovery pipeline; do not create duplicate departments or automatically publish inventory. Category-specific safety, regulatory, shipping, expiry, labeling and supplier requirements remain mandatory, particularly for food, children, health and safety goods. Prioritize useful recurring needs, quality evidence and competitive delivered cost; American manufacturing remains the selective dropshipping sourcing priority, not a restriction on reputable affiliate assortment. Stage expansion according to actual authorized inventory and customer demand. No unsupported product claims or new commercial activation.
